

## -- Module Aims --

1)  To understand the role and function of an OS
2)  To develop a clear understanding of logical components in a computer and how they relate
3)  To understand how data nad processes are represented at machine level
4)  To understand how hardware interacts with software
5)  To develop the practical understanding and skills needed to parallelize and optimize programs on modern processors



## -- Learning Outcomes --

1)  Identify the functionality provided by an OS and how each part works
2)  Explain how data processes are represented at machine level and interacts with hardware
3)  Outlines strategies for achieving parallelism, resource allocation and scheduling within an OS
