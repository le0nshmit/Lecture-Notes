
## -- Module Aims --

1)  Familiarise with software development lifecycle
2)  Develop techniques to produce requirements from abstract problems
3)  Exposure to technical / non-technical skills required to complete a software engineering project


## -- Learning Outcomes --

1)  Identify phases of software development lifecycle (**SDLC**)
2)  Produce suitable functional / non-functional requirements, that when implemented will solve a given problem
3)  Identify and describle operational considerations of software / physical systems / cyber security and networking
4)  Implement a system that fulfills a design specification to solve a given problem
5)  Identify and discuss trade-offs in requirements analysis and production of software versus operational considerations


## -- Coursework Overview --

**Product and Report: [80%]**

This will strutinize the quality of my product, project planning, report content and critical reflection. Marks will be based on the report content, the final product submission and the supervisors experience of your working on your project.


**Poster, Viva & Video: [%20]**

This will scrutinize my ability to present work in various forms: as a poster, a video, a report and by my performance in the viva. Marks will be based on the representative aspects of your portfolio and presentations.
